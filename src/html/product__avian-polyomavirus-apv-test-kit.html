Avian Polyomavirus (APV) Nucleic Acid Test Kit | Molecule Method

Product Introduction

The APV Nucleic Acid Test Kit enables rapid and accurate amplification detection of Avian Polyomavirus (APV) conserved genes in various bird samples, including feathers, blood, tissues, and oral/genital swabs.

By introducing an endogenous internal reference, the nucleic acid extraction and amplification process can be monitored to ensure reliable and accurate test results. This kit is ideal for veterinary laboratories, avian researchers, and diagnostic applications.

Experimental Operation

1. Sample Preparation (Sample Preparation Area)

1.1 Sample Requirements:

  • Feathers: Collect at least 5 feathers from chest, abdomen, or legs (including shaft). Avoid naturally shed feathers.

  • Blood: Use EDTA as anticoagulant. Fresh whole blood is preferred; can be stored at 2–8℃ for up to 7 days or -20℃ for up to 3 months. Avoid repeated freeze-thaw.

  • Tissue: Use fresh muscle or organ tissue (≤100 mg). Avoid skin, tendons, or fascia. Prepare tissue homogenate with 200–500 μL sterile water.

1.2 Nucleic Acid Extraction:

  • Follow the manual of Three Lions Biotech Animal Genomic DNA Rapid Extraction Kit (Part No. DP202) or other compliant nucleic acid extraction kits.

  • Store extracted nucleic acid on ice. Test promptly or store at 4℃ for up to 7 days or -20℃ for up to 6 months.

2. Preparation of Reaction System (Reaction Area)

  1. Take out n+2 reaction tubes: APV positive control, APV negative control, and required reaction solution for samples (n = number of samples).

  2. Thaw reagents at room temperature, centrifuge for 10 seconds, and place in ice.

  3. Add 2 μL of negative control, sample nucleic acid, or APV positive control to reaction tubes (total volume 25 μL).

  4. Mix thoroughly, centrifuge for 10 seconds, and proceed with PCR amplification.

3. PCR Amplification (Amplification and Product Analysis Area)

  • Pre-denaturation: 95℃ for 3 minutes

  • Denaturation: 95℃ for 10 seconds

  • Annealing & Extension: 60℃ for 40 seconds, 35 cycles

  • Fluorescence Detection:

    • FAM: APV specific gene

    • VIC/HEX: Endogenous reference gene

  • For ABI series instruments, add ROX calibration dye if needed.

4. Result Determination

  1. Control Validation:

    • Positive control (FAM + VIC/HEX) Ct < 30 with “S”-shaped curve

    • Negative control Ct ≥ 35 or no “S”-shaped curve
      → Valid experiment

  2. Sample Validation (Endogenous Gene):

    • VIC/HEX Ct ≤ 32 with “S”-curve → extraction/amplification effective

    • VIC/HEX Ct 32–35 or no Ct → sample extraction insufficient or strong inhibitors; reprocess recommended

  3. APV Detection (FAM Channel):

    • Ct ≤ 32 → Positive (APV detected)

    • Ct 32–35 → Suspicious, retest after excluding environmental contamination

    • Ct ≥ 35 or no curve → Negative (APV not detected)

Precautions

  • Strict partitioned operations to prevent contamination; physical isolation preferred.

  • Wear lab coats and gloves; use separate tools in different areas. Change gloves/lab coats as needed.

  • Do not open PCR tubes immediately after amplification; allow cooling.

  • Avoid repeated freeze-thaw cycles; fully thaw reagents before use.

  • Regularly disinfect bench, centrifuge, pipettes using chlorine, ethanol, nucleic acid decontamination agents, or UV.

  • Negative results do not guarantee absence of APV; low quality or inhibitory samples may produce false negatives.

  • Kit is single-use and for scientific research only; not for clinical diagnosis or human use.

FAQ

Q1: What samples can be tested with the APV Nucleic Acid Test Kit?
A1: The kit is compatible with feathers, blood, tissues, and oral/genital swabs from birds. Proper sample collection and storage are critical for accurate results.

Q2: How is nucleic acid extracted for APV testing?
A2: Nucleic acid can be extracted using the Three Lions Biotech Animal Genomic DNA Rapid Extraction Kit (Part No. DP202) or other kits/methods that comply with relevant standards. Extracted nucleic acids should be stored on ice and tested promptly.

Q3: What PCR instrument is required for the APV kit?
A3: Any real-time fluorescence quantitative PCR instrument is suitable. For ABI series instruments, ROX calibration dye may be added if required.

Q4: How are test results interpreted?
A4:

  • Positive Control: FAM + VIC/HEX Ct < 30 with “S”-shaped curve → valid experiment.

  • Sample Validation: VIC/HEX Ct ≤ 32 → extraction/amplification effective.

  • APV Detection: FAM Ct ≤ 32 → Positive; 32 < Ct < 35 → Suspicious; Ct ≥ 35 or no curve → Negative.

Q5: How should the kit be stored?
A5: Store at -20℃, and avoid repeated freeze-thaw cycles. Shelf life is at least 12 months.

Q6: Can this kit prevent contamination?
A6: The kit must be used with strict partitioned operations, physical separation of areas, and proper protective equipment to prevent cross-contamination.

Q7: Is this kit suitable for clinical diagnosis?
A7: No. This kit is for scientific research only and not intended for clinical or human diagnostic use.
