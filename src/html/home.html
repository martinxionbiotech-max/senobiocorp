What we can do

Our Products & Services

DNA & RNA Extraction and PCR Test Kits

Professional extraction and PCR testing kits for efficient processing of plant, animal, bird, and fish samples.

Engineered for research use only (RUO), our kits provide high yield, excellent purity, and reliable performance to support laboratory research and diagnostic workflows.

High Speed PCR Equipment

Professional high-speed PCR systems with patented technology for rapid and accurate nucleic acid amplification.

Designed for research and laboratory environments, offering fast turnaround, high sensitivity, and stable performance to improve efficiency and throughput.

Animal DNA PCR Test

Advanced PCR testing solutions for animal DNA analysis, covering birds, cats, dogs, reptiles, and fish.

Services include sex determination, virus detection, pathogen screening, and molecular diagnostics, providing reliable results for veterinary laboratories, breeding facilities, and research institutions.

Plant and Animal DNA Fingerprinting

Comprehensive DNA fingerprinting services for plant and animal identification, including plant variety verification, seed DNA certification, DNA extraction, and genetic analysis.

Supporting agricultural research, breeding programs, and biotechnology applications with precise, reliable, and high-throughput molecular testing solutions.
